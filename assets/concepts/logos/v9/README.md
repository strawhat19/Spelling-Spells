## Geometry and continuation

- The A keeps its selected `translate(46.4 34) scale(.6)` geometry. Both external leg-parallel wands and the centered gold spark retain their positions and shapes.
- The curved book is layered behind the wands and A. Its entire page content moves with the book, preserving the relationship between text and page edges.
- The original top-row path is unchanged: `M47 196Q77 190 105 202M151 202Q179 190 209 196`.
- The lower left note runs from x60 to x90 at book y216, with smooth four-unit curve excursions; the right note is its exact reflection around x128. This row is narrower than the original x47–105 upper line and sits twenty units lower.
- Both page rows use the same solid purple `#6039D8`, stroke width 6, and rounded caps/joins. No separate faint decorative ink is introduced.
- The A's leg tips are ivory rounded strokes aligned to the leg axes, matching the external wands' pill-tip language. Their stroke width is 24 in the A's local coordinates (14.4 after scaling), with a purple rim around each ivory tip. The previous dark bands and flat ivory tip wedges are removed.

## Palette

Purple `#6039D8`, gold `#FFC857`, lavender `#D8C8F2`, ivory `#FFF9EA`, and night ink `#20134D` are unchanged. Midnight `#17102B` and pale lavender `#F8F4FF` are presentation-sheet backgrounds.
