## Editorial Rules

- Names identify the fictional effect or ability in the specified continuity. A descriptive ability label is not presented as a canonical named spell.
- All summaries below are original. Sources establish the limited claim stated in the evidence column; they do not license copying illustrations, dialogue, or wiki prose.
- Pronunciation is intentionally omitted for this expansion. The inspected sources do not provide an official phonetic transcription. A future approximation must be explicitly labelled.
- Danger, utility and skill are fictional editorial comparisons, not scientific measurements, safety advice, or an official cross-universe ranking.
- The new universes are fictional settings. A future folklore record must identify a cultural tradition and describe a belief rather than asserting supernatural effects.
- `Verified` means the stated effect was supported by an inspected first-party publication or its indexed first-party text. `Needs Review` remains a draft and is excluded from public import until an Owner verifies it. All twelve expansion records initially import as drafts, including the eight researched records, so research status does not grant publication. Publication is a separate Owner decision.
